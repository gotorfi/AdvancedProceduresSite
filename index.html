ABOUT THE PLUGIN

What is Advanced Procedures?

Advanced Procedures is a plugin for MCreator, the mod-making software developed by Pylo. MCreator provides a powerful procedure system that allows you to create custom logic without having to write everything from scratch.

With procedures, you can perform actions such as running commands, checking blocks and entities, working with coordinates, and much more. However, as your projects grow, you may eventually run into situations where the built-in procedure blocks aren't quite enough.

That's where Advanced Procedures comes in.

The plugin expands MCreator's procedure system with a growing collection of additional blocks designed to make more advanced tasks possible, simplify complex logic, and give mod developers more control over their projects.

Whether you need a specific utility, more advanced entity behaviour, additional block interactions, or simply want to make your procedures cleaner and easier to build, Advanced Procedures aims to provide the tools you need.
